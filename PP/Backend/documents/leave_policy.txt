LEAVE POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Annual Leave: 20 paid days per calendar year.
Sick Leave: 10 paid days per calendar year.
Casual Leave: 5 paid days per calendar year.
Planned Leave: Request at least 3 working days in advance through the HR system.
Carry Forward: Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward; casual leave cannot be carried forward.
Emergency Leave: Notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible.
